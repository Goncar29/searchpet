# Font licenses

These files are the latin and latin-ext subsets of three fonts served by
Google Fonts, copied here so the app can serve them from its own origin. All
three are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1:
https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/

The copyright holders and full license of each font are listed on its Google
Fonts page:

|---|---|---|

| `inter-*.woff2` | Inter | https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter/license |
